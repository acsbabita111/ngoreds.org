About REDS

A long-standing rural development society.

Rural Environment Development Society (REDS) is registered with the Registrar of Societies in Bihar. Its official profile records a broad rural-development mandate covering social, educational, environmental, technological and livelihood-related sectors.

This website presents the Society's public institutional profile. Programme pages, reports and project results can be added as individual initiatives are formally documented and approved for publication.
